Methods and source

Data

The atlas shows 10x Genomics Visium and Visium HD data from glioblastoma organoids and primary GBM tissue. Each view shows one Seurat object with all of its cells or spots and all of its genes.

Display

Visium markers are capture spots. Visium HD markers are segmented cell centers, not cell outlines. Tissue can appear without markers when it was not part of the selected object.

Expression values are SCT/data. By default, the top of the color scale is the 95th percentile among cells that express the gene. You can enter a manual maximum instead, which is useful when comparing samples. The scale only changes colors, not the data.

Identity labels and UMAPs come from each source object. UMAPs from different objects are not directly comparable.

Sharing a view

Use “Copy link” to save the current dataset, region, gene, and color settings.
